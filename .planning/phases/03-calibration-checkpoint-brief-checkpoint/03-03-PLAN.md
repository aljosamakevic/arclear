    - EWMA per member over its settled-round debit series with smoothing lambda = 2 / (N + 1); IM_t = q * ewma_(t-1) (margin held ENTERING round t covers round t's realized debit — never lookahead)
    - Warmup: the first N settled rounds per member are excluded from coverage scoring (EWMA state immature)
    - coverage_rate = fraction of scored (member, round) observations with positive debit where IM_t >= debit_t, pooled across the cell's 200 seeds
    - p99_debit = 99th percentile of all scored positive debits in the cell (base units, integer); p99_tail_coverage = fraction of observations at or above p99_debit that IM covered — this is the "does q/N survive the p10/p99 rounds" answer
    - cap_binding_fraction = fraction of scored observations where the uncapped IM demanded a rise of more than 25% over the previous round's IM (i.e., where a +25%-per-round IM rise cap — the procyclicality guard — would bind and leave the member under-margined). Expect this to concentrate in the ramp cells; the number is the point.
    CSV header exactly: n,p_or_ramp,density,reciprocity,seeds,rounds_per_seed,q,ewma_lookback,coverage_rate,p99_debit,p99_tail_coverage,cap_binding_fraction — p_or_ramp is the constant p ("0.95") or the literal "ramp-1.0-0.8". Write docs/sweep/margin-sweep.csv (rates to 4 decimals), console-print a compact summary per flow cell (best and worst covering (q,N)), run the full 200-seed sweep once, and commit the CSV. Do not tune q/N or the ramp to flatter coverage — honest numbers are the deliverable.
